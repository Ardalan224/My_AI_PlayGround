I couldn’t sleep that night, so I made myself a cup of chamomile tea and pulled a book off the shelf. The house was quiet except for the low hum of the refrigerator, and the light from the lamp cast long, soft shadows across the room. I told myself I’d only read a few pages, but the story pulled me in completely.

By the time I looked up, an hour had passed, and the tea had gone cold beside me. The world outside was still and dark, but I felt peaceful, grounded in that small, private moment. It’s strange how sometimes the most uneventful nights end up feeling the most meaningful.
