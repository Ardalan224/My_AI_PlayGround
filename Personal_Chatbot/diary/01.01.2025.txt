I remember waking up a little later than usual and stumbling straight to the kitchen to make coffee. The smell of freshly ground beans filled the air, and I felt like the day might turn out fine after all. I reached for the sugar jar while scrolling through my phone, half awake and barely paying attention.

It wasn’t until I took the first sip that I realized something was horribly wrong — I’d added salt instead of sugar. The taste hit like a slap, sharp and briny. I stood there, grimacing, laughing at myself, and pouring the whole mug down the sink. It was the kind of clumsy start that somehow sets the tone for a perfectly ordinary, human sort of day.
