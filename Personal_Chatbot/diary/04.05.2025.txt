I went to the grocery store that afternoon just to grab some milk and bread, but of course, I ended up wandering the aisles aimlessly. The fluorescent lights buzzed overhead, and the air smelled faintly of citrus cleaner. I stood in front of the cereal section for what felt like ten minutes, debating between something healthy and something that came with a cartoon mascot.

Just as I finally reached for one, a box fell from the top shelf and landed right at my feet. An older man next to me chuckled and said, “Guess it made the decision for you.” We both laughed, and somehow that small exchange made the errand feel less mechanical. I left with the silly cereal — and a reminder that even random moments can feel a bit like connection.
